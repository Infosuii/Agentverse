## 4. M1 (by about T0 + 5 h)

1. **New analysis page (`/`).**
   - **Input.** A large query textarea and three example chips:
     - "What would happen if the UK increased corporation tax from 25% to 28%?"
     - "Cut corporation tax from 25% to 23% from April 2027"
     - "Raise corporation tax to 45%" (this one demonstrates the scope gate)
   - **Parse preview.** Parsing runs on blur, or 600 ms after typing stops. The preview card shows:
     - editable fields: policy type (select), baseline %, proposed %, effective date, horizon
     - the ambiguities, as amber notes
     - a scope badge (supported, partially supported or unsupported) with the reasons and warnings
   - **Rechecking.** Editing a field re-checks scope via `POST /api/policy/scope`.
   - **Options.** Monte Carlo draws (500, 2000 or 5000), research on/off, and use cached research.
   - **Run.** Calls `POST /api/analyses` with `policy_override` set to the edited spec, then navigates to `/analyses/:id/research`.
2. **Analysis shell** (`features/analysis/`).
   - **Header.** Policy description, `ModeBadge`, and a verification badge once that stage is available.
   - **Stage progress bar.** Six stages: parsing, researching, precedents, modelling, verifying, reporting. It is driven by the SSE stream and shows durations from `stage_timings_ms`.
   - **Tab bar.** Tabs are disabled until their data exists. If the analysis failed, show `InlineError` with the error message.
3. **Research tab** (`features/research/`).
   - **Live timeline.** Show `researchSteps` as they stream in. Each step has an icon per kind (plan, search, fetch, extract, dataset_lookup, synthesis, warning, error), a title, a collapsible detail, the query, and `SourceChip`s.
   - **Sections** after research completes:
     - research questions
     - summary, with `[E3]` references rendered as clickable chips that scroll to the evidence
     - evidence list, filterable by topic, verification status and kind. Each card shows the claim, the quote as a blockquote, the value and unit, a `SourceChip`, and a verified/unverified/failed badge.
     - interpretations, each in a rose-bordered card labelled "AI interpretation" and listing the evidence it relies on
     - confounders
     - open questions
     - a sources panel grouped by publisher type, with tier badges
4. **Overview tab.**
   - **Stat tiles** from the central scenario:
     - behavioural revenue in year 1 and year 5, with the p5–p95 range from Monte Carlo
     - business investment % in year 5
     - GDP % in year 5
   - **Labelling.** Each tile shows an `EvidenceBadge` reading "Model scenario". Show the confidence label for low-confidence metrics.
   - **Withheld state.** If `verification.forecast_allowed` is false, show `WithheldBanner` with `decline_reasons` and hide the numbers.
   - **Links.** Quick links to every tab.
5. **History page.** A table of `useAnalysesList()` showing time, query, status and verification badge, with each row linking to its analysis.
6. **Self-check in the browser.** Open the app and run the golden path against the backend in mock mode. Confirm the SSE timeline animates and that every tab placeholder loads.
